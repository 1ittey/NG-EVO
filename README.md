# NG-EVO
### Next Generation Enderal Visual Overhaul


**NG-EVO** is a visuals focused modlist for **Enderal: Forgotten Stories Special Edition**, built to modernize and enhance Enderal's visual presentation while preserving the identity and atmosphere of the original game.

The project focuses on improving the game's **textures, materials, lighting, weather, terrain, vegetation, meshes, shaders, LODs, and overall visual fidelity** through a carefully curated collection of mods, patches, and custom configurations.

> **NG-EVO is a graphics overhaul — not a gameplay overhaul.**

## ✨ Features

- Modernized visual presentation for Enderal: Forgotten Stories Special Edition
- PBR focused textures and materials
- Community Shaders based visual enhancements
- Improved lighting and weather
- Enhanced landscapes, mountains, vegetation, and environmental details
- Improved distant terrain and object rendering
- Custom patches and compatibility fixes
- Carefully generated LODs
- Parallax and complex-material support where appropriate
- Enderal specific fixes and visual adjustments
- A curated and tested visual experience rather than a collection of unrelated graphics mods

---

## 🎯 Project Philosophy

NG-EVO is designed around one principle:

**Improve Enderal's visuals without losing what makes Enderal look and feel like Enderal.**

The goal is not to replace the game's identity with a generic Skyrim visual overhaul. Mods and configurations are selected, patched, or removed based on how well they fit Enderal's environments, architecture, atmosphere, and artistic direction.

Gameplay, quests, progression, combat, and other core gameplay systems are outside the scope of NG-EVO.
