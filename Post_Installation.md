# NG-EVO Post Installation Guide

This guide covers the steps you should complete after successfully installing NG-EVO through Wabbajack.

---

## 🧭 Optional Fast Travel

NG-EVO includes **Enderal SE Dynamic Fast Travel**, giving players the option to use fast travel in Enderal.

Vanilla Enderal does not provide traditional fast travel, as the game was designed around travelling through the world on foot and experiencing its environments and locations along the way.

NG-EVO includes **Fast Travel Patch.esp**, which ensures the fast travel system works correctly with the rest of the modlist.

If you prefer the vanilla approach to travelling, fast travel can be completely disabled by disabling both:

- **Enderal SE Dynamic Fast Travel** — Disable the mod on the left side of MO2.
- **Fast Travel Patch.esp** — Disable the plugin on the right side of MO2.

---

## 🎮 First Launch & MCM Setup

After the installation has completed:

1. Launch the game and allow the shader cache to generate.
2. Once you are in-game, press **F1** to open the **SKSE Menu**.
3. Go to **MCM Memory**.
4. Under **Active Profile**, select **NG-EVO MCM**.
5. Press **Restore Now**.

This restores the recommended NG-EVO MCM configuration.

---

## ⚠️ Important

NG-EVO is designed and configured as a complete modlist.

Avoid changing, removing, or disabling other mods unless you understand their purpose and compatibility requirements. Changes to the modlist can cause visual issues, broken functionality, or instability.

If you encounter a problem after making changes, restore the original NG-EVO configuration before troubleshooting.
