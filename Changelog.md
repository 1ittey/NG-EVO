## [1.0.0] — Initial Release

**Release Status:** Public Release

### Overview

The first public release of **NG-EVO — Next Generation Enderal Visual Overhaul**.

NG-EVO has completed its beta testing phase and is now ready for public distribution.

### Included

- Complete visual overhaul for Enderal: Forgotten Stories Special Edition
- PBR focused texture and material improvements
- Community Shaders based visual enhancements
- Improved lighting and weather
- Enhanced landscapes, mountains, vegetation, and environmental details
- Improved distant terrain and object rendering
- Custom compatibility patches and fixes
- Generated LODs and visual optimization
- Parallax and complex-material support where appropriate
- Enderal-specific visual fixes and adjustments
- Curated visual consistency across the modlist

### Stability & Testing

NG-EVO has undergone beta testing with real-world installations and gameplay sessions.

Issues discovered during testing have been investigated and addressed where possible before the initial public release.

### Distribution

NG-EVO is distributed through **Wabbajack**.

## Versioning

NG-EVO follows semantic versioning where practical:

`MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`

For example:

- **MAJOR** — Major changes to the modlist or its overall direction
- **MINOR** — New features, significant visual additions, or substantial changes
- **PATCH** — Bug fixes, compatibility fixes, and minor updates
