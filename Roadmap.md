# NG-EVO Roadmap

This page outlines planned improvements and future development for **NG-EVO — Next Generation Enderal Visual Overhaul**.

---

## Current Status

NG-EVO has reached its first public release and is available through **Wabbajack**.

The project is now primarily focused on maintenance, compatibility, refinement, and community feedback.

---

## Planned & Possible Future Updates

Future development may include:

- Bug fixes
- Compatibility updates
- Updated third-party mods
- Visual improvements
- Additional patches
- Performance improvements
- Refinements based on community feedback

> This roadmap is subject to change. Items listed here are possibilities rather than guaranteed features or release commitments.
